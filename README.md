# DWELLER

**Light burns. Shadow heals.**

A dark side-scroller in The Seeker universe. You are a small hooded figure hiding from the light, following someone
who came down before you, carrying a crystal that was broken on purpose. Six depths, from ice caves to a beating
heart. Find the six slivers, make the crystal whole, and face what waits at the core.

**Play:** open `dist/dweller.html` in any browser. One file, nothing to install. Works on desktop, phone and gamepad.

**Controls:** A/D or arrows to move · Space to jump · S to roll · E to send your SEEKER · Esc to pause

## The SEEKER

Somewhere in the second depth, a wall is not stone. Behind it sleeps a seeker from SEEKER NET, one of all 2,222,
rolled for your save. Wake it and it follows you. Press E and it flies to a light and covers it, buying you a few
seconds of dark.

Beat the Guardian and the Codex opens: an archive of every seeker you find. Below it lies **The Deep**, eight rooms
that change every run, where your seeker's real traits come alive. Reach the bottom and a new seeker wakes.
Collect them all.
